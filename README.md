## What you're actually buying

BONK launched on Christmas Day 2022 as a dog-themed meme coin on Solana. The launch was unusual for one reason: 50% of the total supply went out as an airdrop to Solana users, NFT collectors, developers and artists rather than to venture funds. The builders stayed anonymous, which is normal in this corner of the market and also means there's no named team you can vet the way you can with a company-backed project.

The supply is measured in the trillions, which is why the unit price has a lot of zeros. That matters for expectations: a BONK price of $1 would imply a market cap no one is seriously modelling.

There's more machinery behind it than the ticker suggests. BonkDAO holds a slice of supply and votes on proposals, the ecosystem includes a DEX (BonkSwap), a Telegram trading bot (BonkBot) and the LetsBonk.fun launchpad, and the project runs periodic burns. The most-cited one, BURNmas in December 2024, destroyed 1.69 trillion tokens worth more than $50 million at the time. Burns reduce supply; they don't make a meme coin behave like a bond.

## The three ways to buy BONK

Almost every purchase path fits one of these:

1. **Spot trading on a centralized exchange.** You fund an account, buy BONK/USDT, and the exchange custodies the coins.
2. **A direct fiat purchase.** Card or bank transfer converts your local currency straight into BONK, usually at a higher fee.
3. **On-chain.** You swap for BONK in a Solana wallet or a DEX, holding the private keys yourself.

They differ in cost, speed and who holds the keys. Which one is "best" depends on whether you care more about low fees or about not trusting an exchange with your tokens.

### Step 4: place the order on BONK/USDT

Search for BONK in spot markets and open the BONK/USDT pair. Two order types matter:

- **Market order.** Fills immediately at whatever the book offers. Fast, and you pay the taker fee.
- **Limit order.** You name your price and wait. If it rests on the book and gets filled later, you pay the maker fee.

On most exchanges outside the top fee tier, maker and taker cost the same. Gate's VIP 0 spot rate is 0.10% for both, so a $500 BONK purchase costs about $0.50 in trading fees. Paying the fee in GT, Gate's own token, drops that to 0.09%.

Before you send a market order, glance at the order book depth. BONK trades in huge quantities, so the spread is usually tight, but on a thin session a large market order can fill across several levels and worse prices than the last traded print suggests.

## Route 3: buying BONK on-chain with your own keys

If self-custody is the point, you skip the exchange account. You hold SOL in a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare (or Gate's own Web3 wallet, which uses MPC key management), connect to a swap interface or a DEX, and swap SOL or USDC for BONK.

Two details decide whether this goes well.

First, the contract address. BONK's Solana mint is `DezXAZ8z7PnrnRJjz3wXBoRgixCa6xjnB7YaB1pPB263`. Anyone can mint a token and call it BONK. Check the mint address in your wallet or on a block explorer before swapping, every time.

Second, slippage tolerance and gas. Solana fees are small, but a swap with aggressive slippage settings can hand a chunk of your trade to a bot. Keep the tolerance tight, and if a quote looks meaningfully off the price you see elsewhere, wait for the next block rather than forcing it.

## After you buy: where the BONK should live

Three reasonable answers, with different tradeoffs:

- **On the exchange.** Convenient, nothing to back up, and Gate publishes 100% proof of reserves verifiable through a Merkle tree. You don't control the private keys.
- **In a Web3 wallet.** You control the keys. Lose the seed phrase and no support desk on earth can recover it.
- **On a hardware wallet.** Cold storage that works with Ledger, Trezor and Tangem devices. Best for holdings you don't plan to move, awkward for active trading.

If you withdraw, you're moving an SPL token on the Solana network. Pick the Solana network in the withdrawal screen, not an ERC-20 or BEP-20 option, and send a small test amount first. A BONK withdrawal sent on the wrong network is gone, and Gate's terms are explicit that on-chain transfers can't be reversed.

## Mistakes that cost people real money

- **Buying the wrong BONK.** Fake tokens with identical names and tickers are routine. Verify the mint address.
- **Skipping the test transfer.** Two minutes and a few cents of BONK beats writing off a whole position.
- **Leaving a large balance on any exchange indefinitely.** Custodial risk is real, even at a platform with published reserves.
- **Using leverage on a meme coin.** BONK has historically fallen more than 50% in short risk-off stretches. Leverage turns a drawdown into a liquidation.
- **Trusting a DM.** Nobody from any exchange support team will ask for your seed phrase or 2FA code. Phishing sites look convincing; type the URL yourself.
